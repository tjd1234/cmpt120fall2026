## Question

Patricia bought these Lego sets:

- An upscaled mini-figure set, with 663 pieces.
- Two copies of a castle set, with 2295 pieces each.
- Three copies of a flower set, each with 210 pieces.

In total, how many Lego pieces did Patricia buy?

Make your output neatly formatted like this:

```
Patricia bought ?? Lego pieces.
```

Replace the `??` with the correct numbers as calculated in your program.

## Sample Solution

```python
print('Patricia bought', 663 + 2 * 2295 + 3 * 210, 'Lego pieces.')
```
