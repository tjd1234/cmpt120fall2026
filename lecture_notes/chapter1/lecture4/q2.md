## Question

You're going on vacation in Tahiti, and you want to get an idea of the
temperature. At this time last year the daily high Tahiti temperatures (in
Fahrenheit) for five days in a row were:

- Day 1: high of 81.5 F
- Day 2: high of 82 F
- Day 3: high of 83 F
- Day 4: high of 84 F
- Day 5: high of 79.5 F

What was the average high temperature over these five days?

Make your output neatly formatted like this:

```
The average high temperature was ?? Fahrenheit.
```

Replace the `??` with the correct numbers as calculated in your program.

## Sample Solution

```python
print('The average temperature was', (81.5 + 82 + 83 + 84 + 79.5) / 5, 'Fahrenheit.')
```
